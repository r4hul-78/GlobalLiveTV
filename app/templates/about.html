About Global Live TV Platform

This platform facilitates legitimate, open access to public broadcast streams across the globe.

Source Attribution

Channel metadata is derived from the community project iptv-org/iptv. We do not own, create, host, or alter any stream content.

Ethical & Legal Principles

  • No Proxying: The backend never re-hosts or tunnels stream data. Playback occurs directly between your client browser and the designated broadcaster.
  • No DRM Bypassing: We do not circumvent any encryption, paywalls, geographic fencing, or authentication systems.
  • Honest Health Assessment: Channels are verified via non-invasive manifest probing to give users realistic expectations about availability.
